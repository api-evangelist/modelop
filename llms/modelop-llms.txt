# ModelOp

> ModelOp is enterprise software for governing and operationalizing artificial intelligence at scale. Its platform acts as an "AI system of record" that lets large organizations inventory every AI asset, automate the model lifecycle from development to production, enforce governance and regulatory policy, and report on cost, risk, and ROI across traditional machine learning, generative AI, and agentic AI.

ModelOp sells primarily to large, regulated enterprises — financial services, insurance, healthcare, manufacturing, and government — and to roles such as Chief AI Officers, CIOs/CTOs, model risk and compliance teams, data science leaders, and security teams. The company is headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. Its flagship offering is the Enterprise AI Command Center, powered by the ModelOp AI Delivery Engine (MADE). The platform is designed to integrate with an enterprise's existing tools (50+ technologies across AI development, execution, data, GRC, ITSM, and security) rather than replace them. ModelOp also publishes a large library of vendor-neutral educational material on AI governance, AI regulations, and ModelOps practices.

## Platform
- [Enterprise AI Command Center](https://www.modelop.com/enterprise-ai-command-center): The core product — a centralized system of record that manages lifecycle, governance, and operational intelligence for all enterprise AI (ML, GenAI, agentic, and third-party/vendor AI).
- [AI Delivery Engine (MADE)](https://www.modelop.com/ai-delivery-engine): The engine that powers the Command Center by connecting 50+ technologies, automating delivery workflows, and letting AI agents plug into the end-to-end lifecycle.
